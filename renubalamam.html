A Letter To Renubala Mam

Dear Renubala Mam...

Mathematics is often described as a subject of numbers, formulas, calculations and logic. But Mam, you taught us that behind all of those numbers is something much more meaningful — the ability to think.

You taught us that when a problem looks difficult, we should not immediately decide that it is impossible. We should stop. Think. Try. Make a mistake. Try again.

And somehow, without even realizing it, those lessons became lessons about life.

There are moments in life when everything feels like a complicated equation. We may not know which formula to use. We may not know which path is correct. Sometimes we may even be afraid of getting the answer wrong.

But the confidence you gave us in the classroom reminds us of something important: Not knowing the answer yet does not mean we are incapable of finding it.

Thank you for every problem you explained. Thank you for every mistake you corrected. Thank you for every time you patiently explained something again when we didn't understand it the first time.

Thank you for turning confusing numbers into understandable ideas. Thank you for showing us patterns where we initially saw only chaos.

Maybe someday we will forget some formulas. Maybe we will forget some calculations. Maybe we will forget the exact questions from our textbooks.

But we will remember something much more important: You taught us not to be afraid of difficult problems.

You taught us that mistakes can be corrected. You taught us that patience produces results. You taught us that practice creates confidence. And you taught us that there is often a solution waiting for those who are willing to keep looking.

Mam, if life were a giant mathematical equation, then teachers like you would be the constants that help us find our way through it.

Your influence cannot be measured by marks, percentages or numbers. It is measured in the confidence of your students, in the questions they are brave enough to ask, and in the problems they are no longer afraid to face.

On this Teacher's Day, I simply want to say: Thank you.

Thank you for being patient. Thank you for being encouraging. Thank you for being there when a difficult equation seemed impossible.

And most importantly, thank you for reminding us that every problem deserves another attempt.

May you always know that your lessons continue beyond the classroom. They live in the way your students think, solve problems, face challenges and believe in themselves.

If every student is an equation still being solved, then teachers are the people who help us discover the variables we never knew we had.

And Mam, you have helped us discover many of them.

With Endless Gratitude ❤️

Thank You,
