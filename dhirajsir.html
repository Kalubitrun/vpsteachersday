✒️ A Little Poem For Dhiraj Sir

Among the shelves where stories sleep,
You taught us dreams are ours to keep.
Beyond each page, beyond each line,
You showed us how our stars could shine.

You opened doors we couldn't see,
And made us believe in what we could be.
With every lesson, word and smile,
You made the journey worth the while.

A library holds a thousand tales,
Of distant lands and ships with sails.
But one story stands above the rest—
A teacher who always gave his best.

So today, Sir, these words we say,
With grateful hearts on Teacher's Day:
May every chapter of your life be bright,
Like a library glowing in the moonlight. 🌙📚
