A Letter For You, Sir

Dear Ravi Shankar Sir...

If I had to write everything you have taught us, one page would never be enough.

You taught us nouns and verbs, sentences and paragraphs, poems and stories. But somewhere between those lessons, you also taught us how beautiful language can be when it comes from the heart.

You taught us that making mistakes while learning is nothing to be ashamed of. A sentence may be imperfect, a pronunciation may be wrong, and a paragraph may need rewriting — but every mistake is simply another step toward improvement.

Perhaps one day we will forget some of the grammar rules. Perhaps we will forget the definitions of some difficult words. Perhaps we will even forget which chapter was taught on a particular day.

But we will remember the feeling of having a teacher who believed that we could do better.

We will remember your explanations. We will remember your corrections. We will remember the moments when a difficult topic suddenly became easy.

And most importantly, we will remember that behind every lesson was a teacher who cared enough to teach it.

Sir, thank you for helping us find our voice through English. Thank you for introducing us to worlds that existed inside books. Thank you for showing us that language is not only about speaking correctly — it is about expressing ourselves honestly.

On this Teacher's Day, I hope you know that your efforts do not disappear when the class ends. They stay with your students — in the words we speak, the stories we write, the confidence we carry, and the dreams we dare to express.

You are not simply a teacher in our timetable. You are a small but meaningful chapter in our story. And some chapters deserve to be remembered forever.

Thank You, Sir. ❤️
