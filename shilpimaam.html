✒️ A Poem For Shilpi Ma'am

You taught us notes, you taught us song,
You showed us where our dreams belong.
Through every rhythm, every tune,
You made our classroom brighter than the moon.

When our voices were afraid to rise,
You gave us courage to touch the skies.
When we missed a note or lost our way,
You taught us to try another day.

Life has its pauses, life has its beats,
Sometimes bitter, sometimes sweet.
But with your lessons inside our heart,
We know every ending can be a brand-new start.

So today, Ma'am, we want to say,
Thank you on this Teacher's Day.
May your life be filled with melodies bright,
Like beautiful music beneath the stars at night. 🌙🎵

🎤 A Song Written For You

🎵 "Your Melody" 🎵

Verse 1

In a room full of voices, you taught us to hear,
You turned every little doubt into courage, not fear.
With a smile and a lesson, with every new day,
You showed us the rhythm to find our own way.

Chorus

You are the melody we carry inside,
The gentle little rhythm that stays by our side.

Verse 2

So this song is for you, with our hearts shining bright,

💌 A Special Message For Shilpi Ma'am

Dear Shilpi Ma'am,

There are moments in life when words simply aren't enough to express what someone means to us.

So today, we would like to express it through the language you understand best — music.

Every song has a beginning, a rhythm, a melody and an ending. But the beautiful thing about music is that even after the song ends, its feeling can remain in our hearts.

That's what your teaching means to us.

We may not remember every single note, every lesson or every word after many years. But we will remember the confidence you gave us. We will remember your patience. We will remember your encouragement. And most importantly, we will remember how you made learning feel like something beautiful.

You didn't just teach music. You taught us how to feel, how to express, how to listen, and how to believe in ourselves.

Years from now, when we hear a beautiful song, perhaps somewhere in that melody, we'll remember the teacher who helped us understand its magic.

May your life always be filled with beautiful songs, peaceful moments, endless happiness and countless reasons to smile.

And may the world always return the same kindness and inspiration that you have given to your students.

🎵 HAPPY TEACHER'S DAY, SHILPI MA'AM! ❤️

With endless respect, love and gratitude,
Your Student 🎶
