✍️ A Little Poem For Vinita Ma'am

Tiny hands and tiny feet,
Little smiles that make life sweet.
Letters, numbers, colors bright,
You make every lesson feel just right.

You teach us how to read and write,
How to share and do what's right.
You teach us kindness, love and care,
And show us that our dreams are there.

With every story that you tell,
You make our little minds excel.
With every smile and every cheer,
You make our classroom feel so dear.

One day we may grow big and tall,
And maybe not remember it all.
But somewhere deep inside our heart,
Your lessons will always play their part.

So on this special Teacher's Day,
There's just one thing we want to say:
Thank you, Vinita Ma'am, for all you do,
For every little lesson, every dream you grew. ❤️

💌 A Special Message For Vinita Ma'am

Dear Vinita Ma'am,

Sometimes the smallest classrooms create the biggest memories.

A place filled with tiny voices, colorful drawings, little hands, innocent questions and countless smiles becomes something much more than just a classroom.

It becomes a place where childhood memories are created.

And behind those memories, there is often a teacher.

That teacher is you.

You don't only teach us ABC and 123. You teach us how to share. How to say "please". How to say "thank you". How to help our friends. How to be brave. How to make mistakes and try again.

You turn ordinary lessons into little adventures. You turn questions into curiosity. You turn mistakes into learning. And you turn a classroom into a place where little hearts feel safe to dream.

Years from now, we may forget some of the worksheets and exercises. We may forget some of the letters and numbers we learned. But we will remember the teacher who smiled at us, encouraged us, believed in us, and helped us take our very first steps into the world of learning.

Thank you for being patient with us.
Thank you for believing in us.
Thank you for making learning fun.
And thank you for filling our childhood with beautiful memories.

May your life always be filled with the same happiness, kindness and warmth that you give to your students every single day.

🌸🌈🧸📚🎨🔤🔢❤️
